The third answer

Keep both. B3 is the mark; A3 is the monogram.

B3 carries the app — icon, favicon, splash, anywhere the brand speaks for itself. A3 carries the name — avatars, the loading state, a stamped mark on a share card, anywhere an initial does more work than a symbol. They share a construction, so they never look like two logos. This is a common arrangement and it costs you nothing.
