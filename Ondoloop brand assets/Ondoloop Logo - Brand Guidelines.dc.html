Ondoloop

The Habits App · Logo guidelines

v2.1 · SEPT 2026
MARK B3 · GABARITO 500

Ondoloop has one mark and one wordmark, and three lockups built from them. In every lockup the mark stands in for the leading O. This document records the construction, the colors, and the sizes at which each form stops working. Every file named here is in the project's logo/ folder.

01 · The two forms

The mark

One ring of two natures: a solid band on the left, three hairlines closing the right. A loop that reads as repetition, and as the O it stands in for.

The wordmark

Gabarito Medium (500) at −0.022em. A warm geometric with generous circular bowls — four of the eight letters are rings, which is what lets the mark stand in for the first one.

02 · Lockups

The mark is the leading O in every lockup. Three shapes cover every placement: the primary for horizontal space, the descriptor cut when the tagline is wanted, and the stacked cut when the space is taller than it is wide. The mark never appears twice in one lockup, and never beside a fully spelled word.

Primary — integrated

The default. Mark set to the cap height of the O it replaces, with the same 1% overshoot above and below the baseline. Use wherever the logo has room to be read as a word.

Descriptor — horizontal with the tagline

The primary lockup with The Habits App set beneath, centered on the lockup — which lands within a unit of aligning to the wordmark's own left edge, so both readings agree. The cap-line tucks up into the descender band; the descriptor clears the p by 32.8 units, so nothing collides.

Stacked, with tagline

The only lockup that carries The Habits App. Tagline set at a quarter of the wordmark's size, tracked to +0.34em, optically centered on the word. A stacked cut without the tagline ships alongside it for tight vertical spaces.

Wordmark and mark alone

Each ships on its own for cases where the other is already present on the page — the mark as an app icon or avatar, the wordmark in a footer beneath a header that already carries the mark.

03 · Construction and clear space

CLEAR SPACE = ½M ON ALL FOUR SIDES

The measure is the mark

All ratios below are in em of the wordmark's size. Call the mark's drawn diameter M. Clear space on every lockup is ½M on all four sides — measured from the ink, not the file's bounding box. Nothing enters it: no rule, no photograph edge, no other logo.

Mark height (= the O's ink height)0.702 em
Descriptor cap-line below baseline0.129 em
Gap, mark to n0.0645 em
Wordmark tracking−0.022 em

04 · Reduction

The integrated lockup depends on the mark reading as a letter. Below roughly 120 px wide the three hairlines start to fill in and the O turns to a blob. There is no spelled-out fallback lockup: below the floor, drop the wordmark and use the mark alone, or set the name as plain text beside it.

200 PX · OK

120 PX · FLOOR

84 PX · TOO SMALL

MARK < 24 PX
USE FAVICON CUT

Minimums: primary lockup 120 px / 32 mm wide. Descriptor lockup 180 px / 48 mm, below which the tagline closes up. Mark alone 24 px; below that the two-ring favicon cut replaces it.

On dark grounds the mark steps up to #8F88FF and the type goes white — base indigo on near-black falls under 3:1. Six cuts ship of every lockup: indigo, on-dark, all-white, all-black, grayscale, and a currentColor cut that inherits from CSS.

06 · Misuse

Don't double the mark

A mark beside a fully spelled word. The mark is the O — one appearance per lockup.

Don't reset the wordmark

Gabarito 500 at the specified tracking, or the shipped file. Never another face, never bold.

Don't tint the O differently

The mark-as-O takes the mark color of its cut. It is not an accent to be recolored per screen.

Don't set the tagline loose

The Habits App belongs to the descriptor and stacked cuts. Don't add it to the primary yourself — use the file.

Don't rotate or mirror the mark

The solid band sits left and the hairlines close the right. Flipped, it stops reading as the O it replaces.

Don't float on mid-tones

Indigo needs a light or a near-black ground. On mid grays use the all-white or all-black cut.
