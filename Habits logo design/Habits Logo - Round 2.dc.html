01 and 04, taken further

Round two · four elaborations each

Both were deliberately bare — round one is for reading the idea, not the craft. Here each one gains structure: internal rhythm, weight contrast, a second reading. The baseline you already saw is included in each row as the control. Contexts at the bottom.

A · the loop H

A1Control

What you picked. Even stems, even weight, one U.

A2Rising bar

The crossbar climbs. Same letter, now it also says progress. The one change that buys the most meaning.

A3Plated stems

Each stem breaks into three plates — the barbell equity smuggled back in, and a rhythm of repetition. Softest at 16px.

A4Return hook

The right arm hooks back over the top — the loop closing. Most distinctive, least legible as an H. Judge it at 24px.

B · body and mind

B1Control

What you picked. Solid disc against three even arcs.

B2Ripple

The arcs thicken toward the centre, so the right half reads as something radiating outward rather than three parallel lines.

B3Annulus

Both halves become bands in one ring: left solid and heavy, right split into three. The counter in the middle gives it air. My pick of the four.

B4Strata

The solid half becomes four stacked bars — days logged. Busiest of the set and the first to fail small, but the most specific to your app.

The favicon problem, and the fix

B loses its three arcs below about 20px. That isn't a reason to drop it — it's a reason to draw a second, reduced cut for small sizes, which is normal practice. Same silhouette, one arc instead of three.
